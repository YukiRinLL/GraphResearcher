# Good Boy (2025 film)

## Lead

Good Boy is a 2025 supernatural horror film directed by Ben Leonberg and written by Alex Cannon and Leonberg, told largely from the perspective of a dog named Indy who is the only one able to perceive the malevolent forces haunting a rural family home. The film marks Leonberg’s feature directorial debut and stars Indy, Shane Jensen, Arielle Friedman and Larry Fessenden, with Kari Fischer and Leonberg producing and Sam Boase-Miller composing the music. Good Boy premiered at the 2025 South by Southwest (SXSW) Film & TV Festival in Austin, Texas and subsequently screened at genre festivals including Overlook Film Festival and Calgary Underground Film Festival, where it was billed as the Canadian premiere. The film is rated PG-13 in the United States and was released theatrically there on October 3, 2025, followed by video-on-demand and digital availability later in the month; it also received a UK and Ireland theatrical release on 10 October 2025 and was acquired by Shudder for distribution in several English-speaking territories. [1][2][23][24][25][29][30][31][39][48][22][15]

## Plot

Good Boy follows Indy, a loyal retriever who lives with his human owner and best friend Todd in the city before the pair relocate to Todd’s long-vacant rural family home. From the outset Indy is wary of the old house, and festival synopses and industry loglines describe him tracking invisible presences in empty corners, perceiving phantasmagoric warnings from the ghost of a long-dead dog, and becoming haunted by visions of the previous occupant’s grim death. As dark entities begin to threaten Todd, Indy alone can see the malevolent forces that swirl around the house and appear intent on dragging Todd into the afterlife. The dog’s efforts to protect his increasingly affected owner form the core of the narrative, with reviewers and promotional materials emphasizing that Indy, who has no supernatural powers of his own, must confront the haunting to save the person he loves most. [22][25][28][29][2][30][3][8]

Critical commentary and festival notes characterize the film as a haunted-house story whose events are aligned with Indy’s sensory and emotional experience, including dream sequences and subjective visions that foreground his fear and attachment to Todd. Reviewers note that the film uses its dog-centered perspective to explore themes of canine loyalty, helplessness and anxiety about losing a loved one to illness or psychological breakdown, framing the haunting as both a literal supernatural threat and a metaphor for the dog’s inability to fully understand or control the human world. [1][3][7][10][33]

## Cast

Festival catalogues and industry listings credit Indy as the canine lead, with Shane Jensen, Larry Fessenden, Arielle Friedman, Stuart Rudin, Anya Krawcheck and Max among the principal cast. Promotional materials and reviews identify Indy as the central dog protagonist, with Jensen playing Todd, Indy’s human owner, while Friedman and Fessenden appear in supporting roles. Calgary Underground Film Festival and Fantasia International Film Festival materials list Kari Fischer and Ben Leonberg as producers, and Alex Cannon and Leonberg as writers, situating them as key creative contributors alongside the cast. [2][23][24][32][8][38]

## Production

Good Boy is Leonberg’s first feature-length film, following earlier work on short films, virtual reality projects and commercials; festival and interview sources repeatedly describe it as his feature directorial debut. Leonberg and Alex Cannon are credited as co-writers, with Leonberg and Kari Fischer producing and Wade Grebnoel serving as cinematographer and Sam Boase-Miller composing the score. Festival materials list the contact entity for the film as Whats Wrong With Your Dog, LLC and name Amy Beecroft as sales agent, indicating the production’s independent status. [23][24][25][32][33]

An interview published by Points of Review reports that Leonberg conceived the project as a high-concept haunted-house film in which the family dog is the main character and the only one able to see the haunting, and that Good Boy was produced on a micro-budget over more than 400 shooting days across three years. The same source notes that Leonberg earned an MFA in directing from Columbia University, later teaching there for more than five years and also teaching undergraduate film courses at Montana State University, before developing Good Boy into his first feature-length film. [25][33]

Press materials and festival catalogues emphasize that Leonberg’s real-life family dog Indy plays the canine lead, with the film’s visuals, editing and sound design structured to reflect Indy’s point of view and fears. Shudder’s acquisition announcement highlights that the film was shot from Indy’s perspective to offer “a glimpse into the hidden world of canine fears” and to explore the unsettling presences dogs might sense in the shadows. [30][31][32][33]

## Release

Good Boy premiered at the SXSW 2025 Film & TV Festival in Austin, Texas, where the festival schedule lists a screening on 8 March 2025 at the Alamo Lamar venue and describes the film as a haunted-house story told from the family dog’s perspective. Following SXSW, Points of Review reported that the film would have its Canadian premiere at the Calgary Underground Film Festival (CUFF) 2025, and CUFF’s archive entry describes the screening as a Canadian premiere. Fantasia International Film Festival materials and Columbia University publicity also reference Good Boy’s inclusion in the CUFF 2025 programme, corroborating its presence on the festival circuit. [25][33][46][39][41][24][40][43]

Shudder, AMC Networks’ premium streaming service for horror, announced in a press release dated 1 May 2025 that it had acquired US, Canadian, UK, Irish, Australian and New Zealand rights to Good Boy following its SXSW premiere, describing the film as a supernatural thriller and noting that the deal was negotiated by Amy Beecroft for the filmmakers and Emily Gotto for Shudder. The press materials also state that Leonberg signed with Altitude to launch international sales at Cannes, positioning the film for further distribution beyond the territories covered by Shudder. [30][31]

In the United States, industry listings and promotional materials indicate that Good Boy was released theatrically on 3 October 2025, with MovieInsider describing this date as a limited release and noting a subsequent video-on-demand and digital release on 24 October 2025. Gateway Film Center’s programme information identifies the film as a 2025 PG-13 title with a runtime of approximately 73 minutes and notes its world premiere at SXSW, but does not specify local screening dates. A review from The Queen’s Film Society is dated 3 October 2025 and discusses a screening of the film on that date, suggesting at least some U.S. theatrical play in conjunction with the national release. [8][22][46][38]

Subsequent coverage reports that Independent Film Company and Shudder would release Good Boy to theaters on 3 October, with a Shudder streaming debut to follow, and notes that IFC promoted the film by releasing an official trailer and scheduling a screening as part of the Scary Movies XIII series at New York’s Lincoln Center in August, accompanied by a director Q&A. An IndieWire analysis states that IFC initially planned a limited run on 3 October but expanded that date to a wide release after the trailer’s strong online performance, citing around 1 million views on IFC’s channels and an additional 1.5 million views on IGN’s account after the trailer’s 18 August debut. [34][44][47][45]

In the United Kingdom and Ireland, Screen Daily and other outlets report that Vertigo Releasing acquired theatrical rights to Good Boy and would open the film in UK and Irish cinemas on 10 October 2025, describing this as a standard theatrical release without specifying screen counts. International Business Times UK adds that after its UK cinema run the film would later be available digitally, including on Shudder, and characterizes the production as told entirely from a dog’s perspective. A Facebook post indicates that local distributor 888 Films International was teasing a Philippine release, suggesting plans for further international rollout, although detailed release information for that territory is not provided. [15][18][19][17]

## Reception

Rotten Tomatoes’ editorial synopsis describes Good Boy as a “visually striking, emotionally devastating horror film” that avoids conventional genre tropes in favour of a uniquely haunting and conceptually ambitious approach. Film Threat characterizes the film as a slow-burn horror work that relies on shadows and silhouettes to build atmosphere rather than frequent jump scares, and praises Indy’s performance, stating that the dog displays more humanity than many human actors. [4][1]

Specialist horror outlets such as HorrorMovieLists and Rue Morgue emphasize the film’s emotional and atmospheric qualities, with HorrorMovieLists describing Good Boy as a hauntingly emotional horror work centred on loyalty, suspense and quiet fear, and Rue Morgue’s CUFF review urging audiences to “sit and stay” for the film. The Hollywood Outsider’s SXSW review calls Good Boy a visually captivating horror film told from a dog’s perspective that offers a fresh, disorienting experience, while concluding that the story falls short of the film’s stylistic ambitions. Collectively, reviewers tend to regard the dog-focused perspective and micro-budget ingenuity as the film’s main strengths, while differing on the originality and effectiveness of its underlying haunted-house plot. [10][40][35][1][5][6]

## Marketing and promotion

IFC Films promoted Good Boy by releasing an official trailer that presents the film as a supernatural horror story about a loyal dog protecting his owner Todd from unseen forces in a rural home, and by hosting the trailer on its branded YouTube channel. Social media posts and promotional coverage note that the trailer, released on 18 August, achieved significant online reach, with IndieWire citing approximately 1 million views across IFC’s channels and a further 1.5 million views via IGN, contributing to IFC’s decision to broaden the planned 3 October theatrical release from limited to wide. [29][34][45]

Points of Review and other outlets identify 3 October 2025 as the target theatrical release date in promotional materials, and The Queen’s Film Society’s review is dated that day and discusses the film’s screening, suggesting that festival and specialty cinema audiences formed part of the early viewer base. An Instagram reel promoting the film claims that Good Boy won a “Howl of Fame Award (Best Canine Performance)” at SXSW and describes Indy’s performance as a standout element, though this specific award is not corroborated by SXSW’s official listings in the cited sources. [8][38][1][25][37]

## Rating and classification

Multiple U.S.-oriented parental guidance and industry sites, including MovieInsider, Parent Previews, IMDb’s parental guide, Common Sense Media and Kids-In-Mind, independently report that Good Boy is rated PG-13 by the Motion Picture Association (MPA) for “terror, bloody images and strong language.” Parent Previews lists a runtime of 72 minutes and a release date of 3 October 2025 alongside the PG-13 rating, and Gateway Film Center’s listing similarly identifies the film as a 73-minute PG-13 title, indicating consistency across exhibitor and guidance sources regarding length and classification. A review from The Queen’s Film Society includes “(R)” in its header next to the date and director credit, but this notation is not explained and may reflect an internal or regional rating rather than the MPA classification. [22][48][49][50][51][46][38]
