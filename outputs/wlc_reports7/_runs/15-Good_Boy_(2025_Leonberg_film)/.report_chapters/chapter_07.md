Several publicly available sources about Good Boy present minor discrepancies or leave certain production details only partially documented.

One documented conflict concerns the films runtime. IndieWire reports the running time as 72 minutes, while the Gateway Film Centers cinema listing gives a runtime of 73 minutes.[5][6] Neither source offers an explanation for the one-minute difference, such as distinguishing between festival and theatrical cuts, and no official clarification is provided in the materials consulted.[5][6]

Sources also diverge on how they credit the films cinematography. Some database entries and credit listings name Wade Grebnoel as the director of photography, with contextual information suggesting that this is a pseudonym associated with Ben Leonberg, whereas other listings simply credit Wade Grebnoel without any indication that the name functions as an alias.[16][18] As a result, the exact relationship between Leonberg and the credited cinematographer cannot be conclusively established on the basis of the available sources.[16][18]

There is a spelling inconsistency in references to the dog performer, who is variously named Indy and Indie in different materials.[16][19] While both sources clearly refer to the same canine actor, no definitive official spelling is established across all promotional and reference documents.[16][19] Relatedly, one director profile describes Good Boy as having been made by a three-person team over the course of three years, emphasizing the compact nature of the core creative group, whereas public credit listings such as those found on film databases and catalogues list a larger number of crew members.[16][18][19] This suggests that the crew of three description refers to a central production unit rather than the entirety of credited personnel, but the exact scope of that formulation is not specified.[16][18][19]

Music credits also show a minor inconsistency. Some materials and professional profiles indicate that composer Sam Boase-Miller worked on Good Boy alongside at least one additional composer, while certain listings attribute the score solely to Boase-Miller.[16][18][21] A professional profile for Boase-Miller notes multiple composer credits on the film, aligning with database entries that list both Boase-Miller and Jeanmarie Riccobono, but not all public-facing descriptions of the films music team reflect this dual-credit situation.[16][18][21]
