Good Boy is a 2025 American horror film written and directed by Ben Leonberg.[1][2][8][14][16][19] The film is produced by IFC Films and is distinguished by being told entirely from the point of view of a dog investigating a haunted house.[2][3][8][9][11][12] The canine protagonist, portrayed by a dog named Indy/Indie, experiences and responds to supernatural phenomena while his human family explores a ghost-infested property.[2][3][16][17][19]

Good Boy premiered at the 2025 South by Southwest (SXSW) Film & TV Festival before receiving a wider theatrical release.[1][2][3][5][6][10] IFC Films acquired and distributed the film, ultimately giving it a wide release in the United States on 3 October 2025 following significant online interest in the trailer.[4][5][8][9][11] The film has attracted attention from trade publications and genre outlets for its formally unusual dog point-of-view concept and its blend of haunted house horror with offbeat humor.[2][3][4][5]
