# Good Boy (2025 Leonberg film)

## Overview

Good Boy is a 2025 American horror film written and directed by Ben Leonberg. The film is produced by IFC Films and is distinguished by being told entirely from the point of view of a dog investigating a haunted house. The canine protagonist, portrayed by a dog named Indy/Indie, experiences and responds to supernatural phenomena while his human family explores a ghost-infested property. [1][2][3][4][5][6][8][9][10][11][12][14][16][17][19]

Good Boy premiered at the 2025 South by Southwest (SXSW) Film & TV Festival before receiving a wider theatrical release. IFC Films acquired and distributed the film, ultimately giving it a wide release in the United States on 3 October 2025 following significant online interest in the trailer. The film has attracted attention from trade publications and genre outlets for its formally unusual dog point-of-view concept and its blend of haunted house horror with offbeat humor.
