Good Boy centers on a family who brings their dog to a reputedly haunted house, where the animal becomes the primary witness to the supernatural activity.[2][3][4][5][8][9][11][12] The narrative is presented entirely from the dogs visual and subjective perspective, including shots at the animals eye level and representations of how it perceives shadows, sounds, and apparitions.[2][3][8][9][11] As the family explores the property, the dog reacts to spectral presences and potential threats, effectively serving as a ghost hunter whose instincts guide the audience through the unfolding haunting.[2][3][7][8]

The film combines tension and scares with moments of comedy derived from the dogs behavior and its interactions with the human characters.[2][3][5] Reviews and promotional materials describe the film as a haunted-house chiller in which the dogs point of view reframes familiar ghost story elements, emphasizing sensory experiences such as smell and motion rather than dialogue-heavy exposition.[2][3][4][5][8] The plot foregrounds the dogs attempts to protect its owners while navigating confusing and threatening supernatural phenomena within the house.[2][3][7][8]
