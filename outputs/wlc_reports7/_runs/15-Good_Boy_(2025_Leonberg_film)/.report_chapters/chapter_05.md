Good Boy had its world premiere at the 2025 SXSW Film & TV Festival, where it screened in the festivals program of narrative features.[1][2][3][10] Festival listings identify the film as a 2025 production and situate it within the horror genre, emphasizing its dog point-of-view structure as a key selling point.[1][2][3][10] Following the festival premiere, IFC Films promoted the film with an official trailer and a dedicated website ahead of its theatrical rollout.[4][8][9][11][12]

The official website and distributor materials present Good Boy as an IFC Films release dated 3 October 2025 in the United States.[4][5][8][11][12] Trade coverage from IndieWire reports that, after the films trailer amassed more than one million views in four days, IFC expanded its plans and elected to give the film a wide theatrical release on that date.[5][11] Promotional pieces highlight the film as a dog point-of-view haunted house horror title and frame the wide release as a response to strong online and genre-fan interest.[4][5][11][12]

Runtime information for Good Boy varies slightly between sources. IndieWire lists the film as running 72 minutes, while cinema listings such as the Gateway Film Center record its duration as 73 minutes.[5][6] Both figures are used in public-facing materials, and no official clarification distinguishing between, for example, festival and theatrical cuts has been provided in the sources consulted.[5][6]
